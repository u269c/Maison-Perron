The mission

Battle Planet is a two-player co-op brawler: one couch, two controllers, friendly fire on. Each player picks a warrior. Any two picks have to make a fun team, because there is no third player to fix a bad match.

Twelve warriors is the sweet spot: enough choice that every player finds "their" fighter, few enough that each one gets real art, real moves and real balancing. Twelve also splits into six natural duos, pairs whose specials set each other up.

The danger is a lopsided cast: eight tanks and no one fast, two warriors doing the same job, or a support nobody wants to play. So we needed ideas first, lots of them, then a way to cut them down without losing the good ones.

The rules of the hunt

Balanced roles

Six roles, two warriors each. Nobody fights over the only speedster.

Duos that click

Every warrior has at least one partner whose special sets theirs up.

No one useless, no one broken

Every strength comes with a real weakness, often one that hurts the partner (friendly fire).

On theme

Retired veterans called back, 80s punk attitude, Quebec flavour, and locals from the Kepler-90 worlds.

One-sentence specials

Each special move fits in one sentence, so a player understands it in a second.
