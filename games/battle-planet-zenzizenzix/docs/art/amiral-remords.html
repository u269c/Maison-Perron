Amiral Remords

Once Lord Villen-Hüüs's general. Now a pirate with a conscience.

For twenty years he commanded the fleet over the gold clouds of Dorée. The day he was ordered to fire on a convoy of refugees, he turned his guns around instead, and his whole crew followed him. They lost an empire and kept a rusty ship, a price on every head aboard, and his rocket peg leg, a souvenir of that day. Now he sails with the heroes, and every broadside is an apology.

« J’ai donné les mauvais ordres pendant vingt ans. Feu ! »"I gave the wrong orders for twenty years. Fire!"
Mutiny Broadside · He raises his sabre and two of his old gunners appear at their cannons. One volley crosses the whole screen; then the gunners stay and fight beside him for 4 seconds.

Strengths

  • Hits the whole screen from behind cover
  • Two extra fighters for a few seconds
  • Rocket peg leg: a short jump-dash

Weaknesses

  • Long reload between broadsides
  • Cannonballs set off barrels and traps near his partner
  • If he goes down, his crew vanishes

Pairs well with Spora: her spore cloud holds the enemies still while the broadside lands.
