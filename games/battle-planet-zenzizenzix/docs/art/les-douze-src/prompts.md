# Les Douze: prompts for realistic paintings

These prompts are for an AI image generator, for example Midjourney, ChatGPT image generation,
Adobe Firefly or Stable Diffusion. They describe the same scenes as the generated cards, written
for a realistic, organic oil-painting look.

**How to use them**
1. Paste the **style block** at the end of every scene prompt, so all 12 look like one series.
2. Use a 4:3 landscape format (Midjourney: `--ar 4:3`).
3. Keep the character descriptions word for word between attempts, so each hero stays recognisable.
4. Save the result you like as `docs/art/les-douze/final/<id>.jpg`, for example `final/marge.jpg`,
   then run `python3 build.py --no-paint`. The card uses that image in place of the generated one.

## Style block (add to every prompt)

> Museum-quality oil painting, realistic anatomy and faces, in the manner of a 17th-century Dutch
> Golden Age master with loose impressionist brushwork. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, rich glazes,
> visible brushstrokes, aged varnish and fine canvas texture. Palette of lead white, Naples yellow,
> yellow ochre, raw sienna, burnt umber, vermilion, madder lake, ultramarine and verdigris.
> Cinematic composition, action in mid-motion, science-fiction setting painted as history painting.

**Avoid (negative prompt):** cartoon, anime, 3D render, CGI, plastic skin, flat colours, comic
outlines, text, letters, watermark, signature, extra limbs, extra fingers, blurry face.

## Recurring elements (use the same words every time)

- **Villen-Hüüs's soldiers:** faceless troopers in dented dark purple-grey armour with a narrow
  glowing red-orange visor slit.
- **The giant planet of Havre:** a huge banded gas giant in ochre, umber and cream, with a rust-red storm.

## I · Amiral Remords (`amiral`)
An elderly sea admiral with a long white beard, weathered face shouting an order, wearing an
ultramarine naval coat with gold braid and epaulettes, a black tricorn hat with a red cockade,
and a brass rocket-powered peg leg glowing blue at the tip, raises his sabre high on the deck of his
ship at dusk. Behind him two gunners in pirate rags fire a broadside of black iron cannons; billowing
gunsmoke is backlit by a low star that silhouettes the admiral with a golden rim light. Villen-Hüüs's
soldiers are blown backwards through the smoke. Rigging, backlit sails and a torn madder-red flag;
the giant planet of Havre fills the sky; embers drift in the air.

## II · Marge la Roadie (`marge`)
A big, strong punk woman in her fifties with a short hot-pink mohawk, a battered studded black
leather jacket, torn fishnet tights and heavy boots, mouth wide open in a joyful roar, leaps
arms-wide from the top of a towering stack of speaker cabinets in a crowded, smoky punk club. Below
her, a huge amplifier slams into the floor among a crowd of Villen-Hüüs's soldiers, and a shockwave
ripples outward knocking them flat. Pink and amber stage spotlights cut through thick orange haze;
sweat, dust and sparks in the light.

## III · The big cyborg (`cyborg`)
A massive hulking cyborg, half his face a cracked steel plate with one glowing red eye and the other
half a scarred human face, his huge body part steel and part black volcanic rock with glowing orange
lava seams, charges forward low and heavy across a volcanic plain, an enormous robotic fist leading.
Behind him a trail of molten lava; ahead, Villen-Hüüs's soldiers are flung into the air and crushed
into the ground. A glowing volcano, rivers of lava, a blood-red sky full of ash and embers; the
figure lit from below by the lava.

## IV · Papi des marées (`papi`)
A wiry man in his nineties with a flat tweed cap, a red knitted cardigan and a flying white
moustache, grinning fiercely, rides a towering stilt-legged creature like a giant wading bird crossed
with a camel, across shining wet tidal flats at sunset. He holds his aluminium walking frame forward
like a jousting lance; a small rocket strapped to the saddle blasts flame and smoke behind. The
creature's foot stomps down in a burst of spray, and Villen-Hüüs's soldiers bounce into the air. The
giant planet of Havre fills the sky and is mirrored in the wet sand.

## V · Ruby Tonnerre (`ruby`)
A lean punk guitarist in her thirties with a tall red mohawk, a black leather jacket and ripped
jeans, rain-soaked, stands on the edge of a wet black cliff holding a red electric guitar high over
her head into a raging storm. Forks of lightning come down and strike a row of stunned Villen-Hüüs's
soldiers on the rock ledge below, lighting them blue-white. Below the cliff, a teal sea steams and
boils; torrential rain, wind, and violent blue-white light against deep teal-black clouds.

## VI · Spora (`spora`)
A large floating creature like a jellyfish whose dome is covered in thick living green moss studded
with tiny glowing flowers, with a translucent pink gelatinous body beneath and long trailing vine-like
tendrils, drifts over a misty marsh at dusk, two soft golden eyes in the moss. A gentle cone of glowing
golden spores falls onto Villen-Hüüs's soldiers whose legs are wrapped in fresh green vines. At the
edge, the old admiral stands healed in a soft green glow. Reeds, still water, fireflies and mist; the
giant planet of Havre faint in a violet and amber sky.

## VII · Docteur Magnéto (`magneto`)
A huge, heavily muscled retired wrestler in a red and gold luchador-style mask and a blue wrestling
singlet with a gold championship belt, bare arms covered in old scars, raises a heavy glowing
electromagnet gauntlet. A tangle of Villen-Hüüs's soldiers, crates and loose bolts flies through the
air toward him along crackling arcs of blue magnetic force. Inside a cluttered space-station market:
paper lanterns, striped stall awnings, steam, and a great round window showing a golden gas planet
and stars.

## VIII · Doc Suture (`doc`)
A grey-haired woman in her sixties with her hair in a bun, a lined, determined face, wearing an olive
military field jacket, a leather medic satchel and an armband with a green cross, throws a white
med-kit that leaves a glowing green-white arc across a rocky moonlike battlefield under a black sky
thick with stars. Parachute flares hang in the sky; red enemy fire streaks from a ridge; across the
field a soldier in green camouflage kneels to catch the kit in a soft green glow. Smoke and dust.

## IX · Demi (`demi`)
A tall hooded nomad stands on the boundary between day and night on a frozen and burning world.
Her long cloak is split down the middle: the left half is on fire, the right half is crusted with
frost and icicles; inside the shadowed hood one eye glows orange and the other ice-blue. A stream of
fire pours from her left hand onto a burning Villen-Hüüs's soldier; frost pours from her right hand,
freezing another soldier inside a block of ice. Behind her the sky is blazing orange day on the left
with a huge motionless sun on the horizon, and deep starry night on the right with tall ice spires.

## X · Bernache (`bernache`)
A furious Canada goose, wings raised high and fully spread, neck stretched forward, beak wide open
mid-honk, realistic feathers with black neck, white chin strap and brown-grey body, standing in a
shallow lake at sunset. A visible ripple of sound blasts out of her beak and sends a squad of
Villen-Hüüs's soldiers tumbling and fleeing through the water. Reeds in the foreground, spray and
drifting feathers; the giant planet of Havre low on a golden horizon.

## XI · The space raccoon (`raccoon`)
A realistic raccoon with a black eye mask and a ringed bushy tail, wearing a patched, oil-stained
ochre space suit and a battered rocket pack, flies over a crowd inside a rotating ring space station,
firing a green energy blaster downward at an angle. A sputtering blue rocket flame and a trail of
smoke behind him; below, Villen-Hüüs's soldiers are hit in bursts of light. The habitat floor curves
up behind him on both sides with small houses and lit windows; stars and a bright star through long
ceiling windows.

## XII · The soldier (`soldier`)
A veteran Canadian infantry soldier in green camouflage fatigues and helmet, in his fifties,
stubbled, face red with rage, roars with fists raised and arms out wide as a visible shockwave
blasts a squad of Villen-Hüüs's soldiers backwards across the steel grating of a mining rig; one of
them flips over the railing. The rig floats in a sea of golden clouds; tall flare stacks burn in the
distance; heat haze, smoke and embers in bright golden light.
