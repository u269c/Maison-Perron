Les Douze

The twelve warriors who chase Lord Villen-Hüüs across the Kepler-90 system, painted in action. Each painting is generated from its brief (open "Painting brief" on any card) and can be repainted with a new seed.

Paintings generated in the page: each scene is laid out as shapes, then repainted with tens of thousands of brushstrokes in a 17th-century palette (lead white, Naples yellow, ochres, umber, vermilion, madder, ultramarine, verdigris), then varnished. Les Productions Maison Perron, 2026.
