# Battle Planet ZenzizenziX — 50 warrior ideas

A long list to pick from: 9 warriors to build, plus the three already in the plan (the big
cyborg, the space raccoon and the soldier). Theme: retired veterans called back to fight,
80s punk attitude, some Quebec/Canadian flavour, and locals from the Kepler-90 worlds.

Each idea: **name** — who they are · health / speed · **special move**.

## Old soldiers and tough workers

1. **Grand-Maman Grenade** — retired demolitions sergeant who knits between fights · Medium / Slow · **Knit Bomb:** lobs a ball of yarn that explodes and ties enemies up.
2. **Le Bûcheron** — lumberjack with a chainsaw arm · High / Slow · **Timber!:** swings a whole tree trunk across the screen.
3. **Sergent Poutine** — retired army cook · High / Slow · **Gravy Flood:** floods the floor with gravy; enemies slip and fall.
4. **The Enforcer** — retired hockey tough guy, still in his old jersey · Medium / Normal · **Gloves Off:** grabs one enemy and lands a flurry of punches.
5. **Zamboni Zed** — ice-rink driver on a rocket-powered resurfacer · High / Slow · **Ice Lap:** freezes a strip of floor; enemies skid off it.
6. **Capitaine Tuque** — Arctic ranger on a hover snowmobile · Medium / Fast · **Whiteout:** a snowstorm; enemies lose track of you.
7. **La Déneigeuse** — snow-plow operator in power armour with a plow blade (a nod to the plow game) · High / Slow · **Plow Through:** pushes everything in a lane off the screen.
8. **Doc Suture** — retired combat medic · Low / Normal · **Field Patch:** heals the partner one heart.
9. **Le Tireur fatigué** — old sniper with very thick glasses · Low / Slow · **Long Shot:** one shot that goes through every enemy in the row.
10. **L'Orignal** — a cyborg moose, ex-army mascot turned soldier · High / Slow · **Antler Charge:** charges across the screen, carrying enemies on its antlers.

## Punks and musicians

11. **Riff Raff Ruby** — punk guitarist · Medium / Fast · **Power Chord:** a sound wave that stuns the row in front.
12. **Benoît la Batterie** — drummer with two sticks · Medium / Normal · **Blast Beat:** hits everything around him in a fast drum roll.
13. **Mohawk Mo** — his mohawk is a blade · Medium / Fast · **Hair Spin:** spins like a top across the floor.
14. **Safety-Pin Sally** — chains and leather · Medium / Fast · **Chain Lash:** yanks a far enemy to her.
15. **Le Roadie** — carries amp stacks for a living · High / Slow · **Amp Drop:** drops a speaker stack; the shockwave knocks everyone down.
16. **Spit Valve** — ska trumpet player · Low / Fast · **Brass Blast:** a cone of noise that blows enemies back.
17. **DJ Vinyle** — throws records · Low / Fast · **Scratch:** records fly out and come back like boomerangs.
18. **Mosh Pit Marge** — big, loud, happy · High / Normal · **Stage Dive:** jumps onto a crowd and flattens it.
19. **Le Tatoueur** — tattoo artist whose tattoos come alive · Medium / Normal · **Ink Beast:** a tattoo panther jumps off his arm and fights for 5 seconds.
20. **Feedback Fiona** — keytar player · Low / Fast · **Feedback Loop:** the noise confuses enemies so they hit each other.

## Locals from the Kepler-90 worlds

21. **Glorp** — a Guimauve cloudjelly in a walking suit · Low / Fast, floats · **Sting Cloud:** a glowing cloud of stings that hangs in the air.
22. **Krakk** — a Fumerolle kettle crab warrior · High / Slow · **Steam Vent:** blasts boiling steam from his shell.
23. **La Mineuse** — a rig miner from Dorée with a drill arm · Medium / Normal · **Drill Dash:** drills forward through enemies and obstacles.
24. **Écho** — a blind echo mole from L'Inconnue who fights by sound · Medium / Normal · **Sonar Ping:** marks every enemy; the partner does double damage to them.
25. **Demi** — a twilight nomad from Crépuscule, half fire, half frost · Medium / Normal · **Flip Side:** switches sides; fire burns enemies, frost freezes them.
26. **Obsidienne** — a lava-rock mercenary from Brasier · Very high / Very slow · **Eruption:** lava bursts up from the floor around her.
27. **Reflet** — a mirror lizard from L'Inconnue · Low / Fast · **Mirror:** copies the partner's special.
28. **Mousse** — a living patch of Havre moss in a scavenged helmet · Medium / Slow · **Overgrowth:** vines grab enemies' feet and hold them.
29. **Le Cavalier des marées** — rides a stilt-legged tidewalker from Havre · Medium / Normal · **Stomp:** the mount stomps the whole floor.
30. **Smog** — a smogwing from Embrume, all wings and soot · Low / Fast, flies · **Soot Dive:** dives through the screen leaving blinding smoke.

## Space and machines

31. **Grand-Papa Robot (Unit 7-B)** — a very old, very obsolete robot · High / Slow · **Overclock:** very fast for 4 seconds, then smokes and stalls.
32. **Pilote Rouille** — La Rouillarde's old pilot, with a jetpack · Low / Fast · **Strafe Run:** calls the ship in for a strafing pass.
33. **Tic-Tac** — a clockwork guard who deserted the Empire on Kepler-80 · Medium / Normal · **Gear Lock:** freezes enemies in place for 3 seconds.
34. **L'Horloger** — the watchmaker who built the rewind watches · Low / Normal · **Echo:** a ghost of him replays his last 3 seconds of attacks.
35. **Capitaine Vieille-Branche** — retired space pirate with a rocket peg leg · Medium / Normal · **Broadside:** a cannon volley fired across the screen.
36. **Général Remords** — one of Villen-Hüüs's old generals, now a traitor · High / Normal · **Call in Favours:** two of his old soldiers fight on your side for a while.
37. **Satellite Sam** — a retired orbital technician with a magnet glove · Medium / Normal · **Magnet:** pulls every enemy into one heap.
38. **Cicatrice** — the last survivor of the first war against Villen-Hüüs · Medium / Normal · **Last Stand:** invincible and stronger while on her last heart.

## Strange, funny and Canadian

39. **Bernache** — a very angry Canada goose · Low / Fast · **HONK:** enemies drop their guard and run.
40. **Bertrand le Castor** — beaver combat engineer · Medium / Normal · **Dam:** builds a log wall that blocks a lane.
41. **La Marmotte** — a groundhog who always knows what's coming · Low / Fast · **Six More Weeks:** dodges the next three hits for sure.
42. **Papi Déambulateur** — grandpa with a rocket on his walker · Medium / Slow · **Walker Rocket:** rams across the screen.
43. **Silence** — a mime · Low / Normal · **Invisible Box:** traps an enemy in a box no one can see.
44. **Le Fantôme du Forum** — the ghost of an old hockey legend · Low / Fast, passes through walls · **Possession:** takes control of an enemy for 5 seconds.
45. **Bingo Bernadette** — never misses Tuesday bingo · Medium / Normal · **B-12!:** draws a ball for a random special each time.
46. **Grosse Bête** — a sports mascot fired for fighting · High / Slow · **Bounce:** inflates and bounces enemies around.
47. **Le Livreur** — veteran pizza delivery driver on a moped · Medium / Fast · **Hot Delivery:** throws pizzas that heal the partner and burn enemies.
48. **Docteur Suplex** — retired pro wrestler · High / Normal · **Piledriver:** grabs one enemy and slams them hard enough to shake the screen.
49. **Météo Monique** — retired TV weather presenter · Low / Normal · **Forecast:** a lightning storm falls on the enemies.
50. **La Sorcière du Marais** — a bog witch from the fogs of Embrume · Low / Normal · **Hex:** turns one enemy into a frog.
