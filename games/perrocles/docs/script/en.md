**HeraQ** — The big one there is called Heracles: “glory of Hera”. Nobody asked him, and he held it against me all his life. You won’t have my glory. You’ll have your own. Pericles… no. Perrocles. “The glory of Perrhaebia.” Your own mountain.  
**The boy** — Perrocles. …(softly, like the phantom said) Perrocles.  
**Hebe** — I’m Hebe. Your aunt, apparently. Here: the nectar. If you drink, you stay here on Olympus, in your father’s forge, for ever. If you don’t, you go home. Both are good.  
◇ Drink the nectar. Stay on Olympus, at the forge.  
◇ Go home. Mama is waiting.  
**Hephaestus** — There’s a workbench for you. I made it twelve years ago. I didn’t know who for. …Yes, I did.  
**HeraQ** — And your mother will visit on Sundays. I’ll see to it. I do the invitations here.  
**HeraQ** — Then I’ll say it for him. Loudly. To everyone. Often. He’ll get sick of it.  
**Hephaestus** — And now and then a limping tinker will come through Oloosson. He’ll mend the pots. He won’t charge.  
**C** — There are stars everywhere now. All of them. I’ve finished counting. …No. I’ll start again.  
**Mentor** — Do you know what I saw, all through this journey? A boy nobody saw. Who ended up making everyone see.
