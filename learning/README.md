# Learning · Pour en savoir plus

Books and explainers about how video games are made, from the Perron family workshop.

| Book | What it is | Language |
|---|---|---|
| **[L'Atelier des jeux](atelier-des-jeux/)**<br>▶ [`index.html`](atelier-des-jeux/index.html) | An interactive book for a child and a parent to read together: where games come from, who makes them, and what each job asks of you. True stories, small experiments to play with, questions to discuss and a 15-minute mission at the end of every chapter. Parts 1 and 2 (chapters 1 to 9 of 15) are written. | FR |

Like the games, each book is one self-contained HTML file: open it in a browser and read. It is
built for an iPad, and every experiment has a full-screen button.
